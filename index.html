Eco-Friendly Tips & Habits

Small changes in your daily routine can make a massive difference for our planet.

💧

Save Water

Take shorter showers and fix any leaky faucets immediately to conserve this precious resource.

💡

Reduce Energy Use

Switch to LED bulbs and unplug electronics when they are not in use (vampire power!).

🛒

Bring Your Own Bag

Say no to plastic bags. Always carry a reusable shopping bag with you.

🍽

Compost Food Scraps

Turn kitchen waste into nutrient-rich soil for your garden instead of sending it to a landfill.

🚴

Sustainable Commute

Walk or bike for short trips — it’s good for your health and the planet.

♻

Recycle Smartly

Sort waste properly and learn your area’s recycling rules.
